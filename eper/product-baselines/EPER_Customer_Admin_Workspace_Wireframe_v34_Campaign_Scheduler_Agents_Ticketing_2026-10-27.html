Approval & delivery rules

  • Draft → review requested → approved or rejected.
  • Approval is tied to the exact content/version and scheduled action.
  • Edits after approval invalidate that approval.
  • Scheduler runs asynchronously; UI shows queued/running/retry/failed outcomes.
  • Retries use idempotency keys to prevent duplicate sends.
