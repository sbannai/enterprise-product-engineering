## Verification instructions

For every row:
1. Open the authorized, controlled BRD version.
2. Confirm the exact requirement ID and requirement text match the row.
3. Record the exact heading/section and page or stable anchor; record document version and approval reference.
4. If the ID or text does not match, record MISMATCH and the actual source ID/locator. Do not silently correct the mapping.
5. If the source is draft, approval-pending, inaccessible, or the locator cannot be established, record UNVERIFIED and raise it to the requirements owner.
6. The verifier records their identity, date/time, and evidence reference. Verification of a locator is not approval of the requirement or acceptance criteria.
