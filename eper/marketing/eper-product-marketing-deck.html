Enterprise workflow platform

Make complex work governed, modular and measurable.

EPER brings application workflows, tenant-aware controls, payment-ready pricing, and human-reviewed AI assistance into one extensible product direction.

Product marketing draft · Claims limited to design intent and documented components
The problem

Business operations are fragmented.

Too many disconnected toolsApplications, files, alerts, workflows and commercial settings are managed in separate places.
Weak control boundariesTenant scope, roles, entitlements and application settings need consistent enforcement.
Slow manual follow-upLong-running actions need asynchronous execution, retries and visible status.
Unreviewed automation riskAI assistance must preserve human approval for external actions.
The EPER approach

One modular platform. Clear ownership.

Platform AdminProduct catalogue, localized market pricing, plans/tariffs, customers, application assignment, entitlements and client access.
Customer AdminApplication-specific settings, integrations, notifications, workflows and enabled add-ons.
Governed executionAsync jobs, idempotency, review workflows and durable audit as core design principles.
Responsive experienceAdmin and customer surfaces designed for desktop, tablet and mobile use.
Localized commercial model

Market price is not display currency.

  • Product Admin maintains explicit approved price versions by product and market.
  • Locale controls language and formatting—not contract value.
  • Market selection chooses an approved market price; it does not silently convert via FX.
  • Accepted price snapshots protect existing agreements from silent repricing.
AI with human oversight

Assist first. Act only with approval.

CRM, Sales and Marketing agents can support draft creation and review workflows. External publishing, outbound messages and CRM writes remain gated by authorization, explicit approval and execution-time checks.

DraftGenerate a suggested campaign, follow-up or CRM summary.
ReviewIndependent reviewer approves or rejects.
ExecuteAuthorized adapter checks policy and idempotency.
AuditRecord outcome without exposing secrets.
Integration direction

Configure each application in its own context.

Payments, WhatsApp/messaging, storage and document sharing, video conferencing, alerts and workflows belong in the selected application's Settings area, constrained by customer entitlements and role permissions.

Provider availability depends on implemented adapters, credentials, environment and verification; a configuration screen is not proof of a live integration.
Delivery with evidence

Build modularly. Verify before release.

  • Product-level BRD, HLD, LLD and SRS provide a common baseline.
  • CI, technical simulation and Business UAT are distinct evidence stages.
  • Durable persistence, authorization, audit and payment consistency are release gates.
  • No certification, SLA, live provider, or Business UAT claim without evidence.
